# Heavy machines

Big machines you drive: a drill rig that digs tunnels and shafts, and a tree cutter that takes down a whole tree at once.

Try them: `./mc kit heavy-machines` (add `--craft` for what crafts more).

## The drill rig

1. Put the drill rig down on the ground, and use it to get on.
2. Hold **W** and look where you want to drill: ahead, up, or down. When a block is in front of the drill, it cuts a 3×3 hole and drives in. Sneak to get off.
3. Everything it digs goes into your inventory. Whatever doesn't fit waits next to the rig.

- **Ahead**, the tunnel's floor stays level with the rig, so you can drive straight through.
- **Down**, it cuts the floor under it and sinks into the shaft.
- **Up**, it cuts the ceiling and climbs. It holds on in the shaft until you look down, or until it's standing on something again. If you get off, it drops down.
- **Not in water or lava.** It stops when it stands in water or lava, or when there's water or lava in front of the drill or right around it, so it never opens a tunnel into a lake.
- It can't cut bedrock, barriers, portals, or command blocks.

**Bigger drill heads.** Use a drill head on the rig to put it on; the old head drops next to the rig. Bigger heads cut wider but slower.

| Drill head | Cuts | Craft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drill Head | 3×3 (every rig comes with one) | 5 iron ingots around a diamond |
| Big Drill Head | 5×5 | a drill head with 4 diamonds |
| Giant Drill Head | 7×7 | a big drill head with 4 diamonds and 4 iron blocks |

Sneak and hit an empty rig to pick it up: its drill head comes back too.
Craft a rig from a drill head, 4 iron blocks, a furnace, and 3 iron ingots.

## The tree cutter

Put it down, get on, and drive into a tree with **W**. It cuts down the whole tree: the trunk, the branches that go sideways, and the leaves. The logs, saplings, and apples go into your inventory.

It only cuts trees. Logs without leaves, like a log cabin, are someone's building, so it leaves them alone. It doesn't work in water or lava either.

Craft one from a diamond axe, 4 iron blocks, a furnace, and 3 iron ingots.

## Only a person can check

How the machines look and how driving them feels: the tests drive them with simulated players, which can't steer.

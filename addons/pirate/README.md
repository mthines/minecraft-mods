# Pirate

Be a pirate: a saber that sets mobs on fire, a ship to sail, and pirate friends who join your crew.

Get everything with `./mc kit pirate`: the saber, a ship, and 3 pirate friends. More pirates: `./mc kit pirate-crew`. Another ship: `./mc kit pirate-ship`.

## The pirate saber

Hit a mob with it and the mob catches fire. Craft it from 2 iron ingots, a gold ingot, a stick, and blaze powder.

## The pirate ship

1. Look at water and use the ship. It goes on the water, pointing the way you look.
2. Use the ship to climb aboard. The first one aboard is the captain, at the steering wheel: only the captain steers. When the captain gets off, the next one aboard moves up to the wheel.
3. **W** sails forward, **S** backs up, **A** and **D** turn. Sneak to get off.
4. Sneak and hit an empty ship to put it back in your inventory.

It has room for the captain and 7 more: friends, or your pirate crew. Pirates always leave 2 seats for friends, and never take the wheel. It only sails on water.
Craft one from 3 white wool (the sail), a stick (the mast), and 3 oak planks (the hull).

## Pirate friends

Use a Pirate Friend to bring a pirate. They get a pirate name and join the crew of whoever brought them:

- they follow you, and fight monsters near you (not creepers)
- they fight whatever hurts you, and whatever you fight
- they jump aboard when you get on your ship, and get off when you do
- their sabers set mobs on fire too

A pirate who isn't in anyone's crew joins yours for a gold ingot.
Craft a Pirate Friend from an emerald, a gold ingot, and leather.

Some things only a person can check: how the ship and the pirates look, and whether steering feels right.
The tests (`./mc test pirate`) check the rest.
